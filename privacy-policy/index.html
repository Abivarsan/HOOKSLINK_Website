Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Registered Company Number 15993172 (England & Wales)

1. Commitment to Data Protection

Hookslink Digital Marketing Ltd ("Hookslink", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting and respecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and protect your personal data when you visit our website (hookslink.com) or contract our services in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Information We Collect

To deliver our digital growth and web development services, we may collect:

  • Personal Identification Data: Full name, corporate email address, telephone contact number, company name.
  • Technical & Usage Data: Internet protocol (IP) address, browser client details, operating system, pages visited, and interaction durations.
  • Marketing & Communication Data: Inquiries submitted via our consultation forms, correspondence records, and communication preferences.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under the UK GDPR, we rely on the following lawful bases:

  • Consent: Where you have explicitly opted in (such as submitting an inquiry or requesting a digital audit).
  • Contractual Necessity: Where processing is necessary to execute an agreement or provide requested agency services.
  • Legitimate Interests: To analyze and refine our service performance, defend legal claims, and secure our network infrastructure.

4. Data Security & Sharing

We enforce appropriate technical and organizational encryption measures to secure your personal data. We will never sell, lease, or rent your personal information to third parties. Data is shared solely with trusted service providers necessary to fulfill agency operations (e.g., secure hosting infrastructure).

5. Your Legal Rights

Under UK data protection laws, you possess rights including the right of access, rectification, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), restriction of processing, and data portability. To exercise any of these statutory rights, please contact our Data Representative at info@hookslink.com.
